Eclair Pie

wikimedia Commons Attribution 2.0 Generic No changes made.

Yummy eclair pie...so good!

Ingredients

  • Instant vanilla pudding mix
  • Cool Whip
  • Graham crackers
  • Chocolate frosting

Steps

  1. Make instant pudding
  2. Once pudding is ready, fold in one container of Cool Whip
  3. In a large dish, add layer of craham crackers
  4. Add layer of pudding mix
  5. Repeat steps 3 and 4 until all pudding is added
  6. Melt chocolate frosting in microwave or stove top until liquified
  7. Pour over top layer of pudding
  8. Refrigerate until frosting is once again solid
  9. Serve and be full of joy!
