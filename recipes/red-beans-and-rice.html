Easy Red Beans and Rice

“red beans and rice” by Paul Goyette, CC BY-NC-SA 2.0 No changes made.

Nothing beats a good bowl of red beans and rice

Ingredients

  • Red Beans from can (Blue Runner)
  • Rice
  • Onion, chopped
  • Bell Pepper, chopped
  • Zatarain’s Red Beans and Rice Seasoning
  • Olive Oil

Steps

  1. Cook desired rice amount
  2. In a large pot, combine, tablespoon of olive oil, onions, and bell peppers. Cook until onions are slightly golden brown.
  3. Add red beans and one-half to one can of water.
  4. Bring to a light boil, stirring occasionally.
  5. Lower heat and simmer 10 minutes.
  6. Stir in cooked rice and serve.
